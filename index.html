Why Choose a Kneeling Chair?

Science-backed benefits for your back and productivity

🏃

Active Sitting

Engages your core and leg muscles while you work, burning up to 4x more calories than a standard chair.

🦴

Spinal Alignment

Naturally tilts your pelvis forward, aligning your spine in its healthy S-curve and reducing disc compression.

😌

Pain Relief

Clinically shown to reduce lower back and hip pain by distributing body weight across multiple pressure points.

🧠

Better Focus

Improved oxygen flow from better posture leads to sharper concentration and reduced afternoon energy slumps.

⚖️

Weight Distribution

Spreads 20% of your body weight to your shins and knees, taking the load off your lower back entirely.

💪

Core Strengthening

Daily use gradually strengthens abdominal and back muscles, making good posture effortless over time.
